Our story

Creole roots, an Italian wood oven, one stretch of sand.

Kafe Kreol sits directly on Anse Royale beach, where a Creole kitchen and a wood-fired oven share the same terrace. It started as a simple idea: cook the island's seafood the way families here have always cooked it, and bring in a pizza oven for the days you just want something familiar with your feet in the sand.

Every curry paste, every dough, every rum infusion is made on site. Nothing arrives frozen, and nothing is rushed — least of all the sunset, which the whole terrace tends to pause for around 6pm.
